Thursday 24 Dec

The Night Bus

Dinner at home, sleeper bus at 21:15, sleep rolling south — Chennai by 08:20.

  1. 19:30

    Dinner at home food

    Early dinner — the bus has no pantry. Pack snacks + 1 litre of water each for the night; morning kit (brush, facewash) in the small bag.

  2. 20:30

    Boarding point pack

    FlixBus boarding — check the exact point on the ticket. Be there by 20:45 for 21:15. 10 lower berths in 5 doubles — screenshot berth numbers.

  3. 21:15

    Board the sleeper to Chennai bus

    About 11 hours overnight. Sleep. Use the mid-night washroom halt. Valuables stay on you.

  4. 08:20

    Chennai — CMBT / Kilambakkam arrive

    Alight at Kilambakkam if the bus halts there — closer to Kanchi. Face-wash at the station washrooms, then the Kanchi bus.

Why the bus works: no 60-day quota race, no Warangal midnight run, and you board in the city. If the bus is not rolling by 21:15 sharp, every delay eats the Friday bath. Merges are rare in Christmas week; keep the 22:40 in mind till you are rolling.
Friday 25 Dec

Kamakshi Day

Hotel by 10:30, bath and breakfast, Varadharaja afternoon, Kamakshi evening, two silk stops.

  1. 08:30

    Bus to Kanchipuram bus

    About 2 hours from CMBT (~₹70), ~1.5 hours from Kilambakkam. Hotel ~10:30 — early check-in negotiated at booking. Autos to the hotel, ₹80 each, about 3 per auto.

  2. 10:30–11:30

    Freshen up — bath bath

    Full bath and change after the bus — the only proper freshen-up between Friday morning and Sunday 03:30. 60 minutes at 3 turns per bathroom (about 15 minutes a turn); last turns go straight to breakfast.

  3. 11:30–12:15

    Brunch, hotel (main meal) food

    One big late-morning meal, ₹150 each — the evening runs on it (dinner 20:30). Then rest — temples are shut till 16:00.

  4. 12:30–15:00

    Rest rest

    Sleep properly — the only real rest of the trip. Shops stay open through the temple-shut hours.

  5. 15:00–15:45

    Kanchi shopping — silks, round 1 shopping

    First silk stop on the temple streets, autos from the hotel. Fix one street and one meeting point; buyers list ready. Back in time for Varadharaja.

  6. 16:00–17:00

    Varadharaja Perumal darshan

    Autos (~3 km each way). Margazhi crowds are possible this week — if the queue overflows, cut to a 30-minute darshan; Kamakshi is protected. Back by 17:15.

  7. 17:30–19:00

    Kamakshi temple darshan

    Open until about 21:30 on Fridays. Everyone in one queue, so allow the full 90 minutes — an hour for the queue, buffer for the crowd.

  8. 19:00–19:15

    Golden chariot don't miss

    Goes round the temple around 19:00. Watch the start of the round — do not leave before it appears.

  9. 19:15–20:00

    Kanchi shopping — silks, optional shopping

    45 minutes on the temple streets — fix one street and one meeting point so nobody scatters. Decide buyers beforehand; anyone tired heads back to rest before dinner.

  10. 20:30

    Dinner, Kanchipuram food

    ₹180 each. Then walk back and sleep — no phones after 21:15.

  11. 21:15

    Sleep rest

    A full night — 8 hours. The only full night of the trip.

Late-bus variant (arrival after 12:00): bath on arrival, lunch shifts +60, rest shrinks. Cut in this order — shopping, Varadharaja, bath→face-wash. Kamakshi and the 19:00 chariot are protected last.
Saturday 26 Dec

Sripuram, Then the Walk

Golden temple in the morning, Tiruvannamalai by evening, a short sleep — then 14 km on foot.

  1. 05:30–06:30

    Wake, freshen up — bath bath

    Morning bath and pack — wake in room order so the bathrooms pipeline from 05:30. 60 minutes at 3 turns per bathroom (about 15 minutes a turn); last turns finish by 06:45. Checkout is 07:45 and today ends at 03:30 Sunday.

  2. 07:00

    Breakfast, hotel food

    Ordered the night before. ₹100 each. If the hotel does not do breakfast, the tiffin shops near the bus stand open at 06:30.

  3. 07:45

    Check out pack

    Bags stay with you from here until 16:10. Autos to the bus stand, about 3 per auto.

  4. 08:00

    Bus to Vellore bus

    1.5–2 hours, ₹55, every 30–60 minutes. Get off at Vellore new bus stand.

  5. 09:50

    Autos to Sripuram autos

    20 minutes, ₹150 an auto, about 3 per auto. Leave phones and bags in the free cloakroom — nothing goes past the gate, and bags take a while.

  6. 10:15–12:00

    Sripuram darshan darshan

    Open 08:00–20:00, free. The star path is about 1 km each way, barefoot. If running late, take the ₹100 shorter queue — protect the 12:45 autos, not the darshan.

  7. 12:00

    Lunch at Sripuram food

    The annadanam hall serves free meals through the middle of the day. Eat quickly, autos leave at 12:45. If it is shut, the veg restaurants on the road back to the bus stand are ₹150 each.

  8. 12:45

    Autos to the bus stand autos

    20 minutes. Fill water bottles here — the next stop is 16:00.

  9. 13:15

    Bus to Tiruvannamalai bus

    2.5–3 hours, ₹180, every 30–45 minutes. This is the last bus that gets you in before dark.

  10. 16:00

    Tiruvannamalai arrive

    Hotel near the east Rajagopuram, walking distance from where the walk starts.

  11. 16:10–16:50

    Check in, freshen up, change face wash

    Quick face wash and into fresh walking clothes after the bus — dinner is at 17:00 sharp, then sleep. Count the bathrooms on arrival — that number is what the rest of the night is built on.

  12. 17:00

    Dinner at the hotel food

    Call the order through from the bus around 15:00 so food is on the table at 17:00. ₹150 each. Rice, sambar, curd, vegetables — nothing fried, nothing heavy. This is the meal you walk 14 km on.

  13. 17:30–21:00

    Sleep, 3½ hours rest

    Set two alarms. The next real sleep is 08:15 on Sunday.

  14. 21:00

    Wake, tea, get ready ready

    Torch, one litre of water each, slippers, and a biscuit in the pocket for 03:00.

  15. 21:30

    Start Girivalam 14 km

    From the east Rajagopuram. 14 km, keep the hill on your right. 5–6 hours for the group — the pace that counts is the slowest person's.

Delay rule: if the group is not on the Vellore bus by 08:30, cut Sripuram — take the direct bus to Tiruvannamalai instead (about 3 hours) and bank the extra rest before the walk. A delayed Sripuram squeezes the 13:15 bus and the whole night unravels; the walk matters more.
Sunday 27 Dec

Darshan + Night Bus

Bathe, 05:30 darshan, rest the whole day — then one sleeper straight home.

  1. 02:30–03:30

    Back at the hotel finish

    Five hours if you keep a steady pace, six if you stop everywhere. People arrive spread over that hour, so the bathing queue starts as each room lands.

  2. 03:30–05:10

    Bath and change bath

    Bathrooms rotate, about 15 minutes a turn. This is the block that decides the 21:30 start. If bathrooms work out to fewer than one per room: dinner 16:30, sleep 17:00–20:30, wake and start the walk at 21:00.

  3. 05:20

    Walk to the temple temple

    Ten minutes from the east gate. Clean clothes, no bags, no phones.

  4. 05:30–07:30

    Arunachaleswarar darshan darshan

    Temple opens 05:30. The group will not stay together in the queue — fix a meeting point outside and a time, 07:30.

  5. 07:30

    Breakfast, Car Street food

    The veg places near the east gate are open by 06:30. Idli, pongal, vada, filter coffee, ₹80 each. A large group at one sitting is a lot — split across nearby shops, or send someone ahead to order.

  6. 08:15–10:00

    Sleep, 1¾ hours rest

    Back in the same rooms. This is the sleep you do not get between the walk and darshan.

  7. 10:00–10:15

    Face wash rest

    Quick face wash — rooms are held till 17:00 (day rate negotiated at booking), bags stay put.

  8. 10:15–13:00

    Rest in rooms rest

    Sleep off the walk. Rooms held till 17:00 — this rest is what the night walk costs.

  9. 13:00

    Lunch, hotel / town food

    ₹150 each. Eat well — bus dinner halt is ~20:00.

  10. 14:00–16:30

    Rest rest

    Final sleep top-up before the bus.

  11. 16:30

    Wash, pack, check out pack

    Bags together. Autos to the boarding point.

  12. 17:00

    Boarding point pack

    Confirm the exact point on the ticket; be there 30 minutes early. 10 lower berths.

  13. 17:30

    Board the direct sleeper to Hyderabad bus

    About 12–13 hours overnight. Dinner halt ~20:00 at a highway restaurant — eat there. Then sleep.

Sat 21:30 → Sun 03:30

The 14 km Walk

Girivalam, under a moonless sky

Start at the east Rajagopuram, keep the hill on your right, one road all the way round. 5–6 hours — the stops set the pace, not the walking. The last full moon was 23–24 December, so don't expect the road lit or crowded.

  • Start 21:30 sharp — six hours walking + 90 min of baths = the 05:30 temple gate. Only a 21:30 start adds up.
  • Bathe at the hotel, not the temple. Clean clothes for darshan; nobody goes in dusty.
  • Halfway rule: not halfway by 00:30 → turn back. The loop back is still the full 14 km.
  • Stay together-ish: one person with the slowest walker, regroup at halfway. The group strings over a kilometre.
  • Carry: 1 litre of water each, a torch, a biscuit for 03:00. Slippers — it's asphalt.
  • Walk only if fit. The day started at 05:30 on a 3½-hour nap — anyone unwell sits it out. No shame in it.
  1. pace

    Time 5–6 hrs

    14 km at 2.5 km/h is 5.6 hours — and the stops at the eight lingams and the two Ganesh shrines set the pace, not the walking.

  2. before

    Sleep 17:30–21:00 first rest

    Three and a half hours, then walk. There is no gap for sleep between the walk and darshan — it comes afterwards, 08:15 to 10:00.

Checklist

Before You Go

Eleven things worth knowing.

  1. Book the bus berths now. Christmas sleepers sell out; FlixBus inventory is open while most operators load ~30 days out. 10 berths, lower deck, one bus.
  2. Book the return bus too. Same ~24 Nov window as outbound — same operator if possible, one thread for all 20 berths.
  3. FlixBus merges half-full trips. Prefer the mid-pack 21:15; screenshot berth numbers; keep the 22:40 in mind till you are rolling.
  4. The bathrooms are the real constraint. Book enough rooms at Tiruvannamalai for 3 per bathroom — confirm bathroom count in writing. Below one bathroom per room, the walk moves to 21:00.
  5. Tell both hotels the same two things: breakfast at the time in the table, and the checkout time you need.
  6. Return dinner is the ~20:00 highway halt. Eat there properly and carry night snacks — nothing after.
  7. Buses have zero buffer. A 2-hour-late bus eats the bath — same cut order as the late-bus variant; never Kamakshi or the chariot.
  8. Agree a meeting point before the darshan queue. The group will be separated by the crowd. Outside the east gate at 07:30 is enough.
  9. Sripuram: no phones, no bags, and the star path is barefoot. Free cloakroom at the gate — bags will take a while, so allow for it.
  10. Kanchipuram temples close about 12:15–16:00. That is why Friday is Varadharaja 16:00, then Kamakshi and the chariot — and why the 11:30 brunch is the main meal.
  11. The return bus boards in Tiruvannamalai town, not at any station. Confirm the exact point on the ticket; be there 30 minutes early.
